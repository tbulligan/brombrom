# Privacy Policy for BromBrom Manager

**Effective Date:** May 28, 2026

Tomaso Bulligan ("we", "our", or "us") operates the BromBrom Manager mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Application.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

**We do not collect, store, or share any personal data.** 

BromBrom Manager is designed with privacy in mind:
* **No User Accounts:** You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide any personal details (such as name, email, or phone number) to use the app.
* **No Tracking or Analytics:** We do not use third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertising networks inside the app.
* **No Location Tracking:** The application does not collect, record, or track your GPS location.

## 2. Network and Internet Usage

The application requires Internet access (`android.permission.INTERNET`) solely for the following technical function:
* **Checking for Updates:** The app sends a standard HTTP GET request to the public GitHub API (`https://api.github.com/repos/tbulligan/brombrom/releases/latest`) to check if a new version of the map data (`BromBrom.osf`) is available for download.

During this request, standard server logs (such as your IP address) may be processed by GitHub as the hosting provider. You can refer to [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement) for more information on how they handle network traffic.

## 3. Children's Privacy

Our application does not target children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from anyone under the age of 13.

## 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
