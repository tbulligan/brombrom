Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the BromBrom Manager Android application and the BromBrom website. We believe in absolute privacy and transparency.

1. Data Collection

The BromBrom Manager app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. We do not use any tracking SDKs (like Google Analytics, Firebase, or Facebook Pixel). We do not require you to create an account, and we do not track your location.

2. Location Data

The BromBrom Manager app itself does not use your device's location. The offline map data (.osf) downloaded by our app is meant to be used within the third-party OsmAnd application. OsmAnd uses your device's GPS to provide navigation, but this data remains on your device and is not shared with us.

3. Network Usage

The BromBrom Manager app connects to the internet solely for the following purpose:

  • To communicate with the public GitHub API to check for map updates.
  • To download the map updates (.osf files) directly from GitHub releases.

4. App Permissions

The app requests the following permissions for specific functionalities:

  • Notifications: To alert you when a new map update is available (checked in the background).

5. Third-Party Services

While our app does not track you, we rely on the following third-party service:

  • GitHub: Used to host our releases and check for updates. Their privacy policy applies when the app interacts with their servers.

6. Changes to this Policy

If we decide to change our privacy policy, we will post those changes on this page. Since we collect no data, we have no way to notify you directly of changes.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us by creating an issue on our GitHub repository.
